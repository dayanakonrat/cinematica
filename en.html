FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can we bring our own food and drinks?

Yes, you can bring any food and drinks. Or order delivery straight to the room, with no extra fee.

What can we watch?

Anything available online: ad-free films and series in good quality, with new releases right after they come out. You can also play a video from your own link.

Can we extend the session?

Yes, each extra hour costs 1 250 ₸ per person.

Can we smoke hookah?

Yes, we have hookahs, from 6 000 ₸.

Do you have PlayStation or karaoke?

No, we are all about cinema: a big screen and great sound.

How can we pay?

Cash, Kaspi transfer or Kaspi Red. The booking deposit is paid via Kaspi.

What are your opening hours?

From 14:00 until the last booked guest. If needed, we can open earlier, from 12:00: just arrange it with the administrator in advance.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Free of charge with 24 hours’ notice. After that the deposit is non-refundable, but you can reschedule once.

Is there an age limit?

Before 21:00 entry from age 16, after 21:00 only 18+.
